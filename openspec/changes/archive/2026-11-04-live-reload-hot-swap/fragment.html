Hot-Swap vs. Fallback Decision Flow

Mermaid Architecture 
flowchart TD
  Event["SSE 'reload' Event from Dev Server"] --> Baseline["Fetch raw next HTML"]
  Baseline --> SigCheck{"Signature check:\n<head> assets or inline <script> changed?"}
  
  SigCheck -- "Content only (<main> markup)" --> LoadIdio["Load Idiomorph (CDN)"]
  LoadIdio --> Morph["Morph <main> in-place\nSkip canvas/widgets\nKeep theme, scroll & state"]
  Morph --> Reinit["Call reinitReportContent()\nRehydrate icons, TOC & diagrams"]
  
  SigCheck -- "Scripts / Head modified" --> Fallback["Save scroll to sessionStorage"]
  Fallback --> FullReload["window.location.reload()"]
  FullReload --> RestoreScroll["Restore scroll after load\nTheme already restored pre-paint"]
