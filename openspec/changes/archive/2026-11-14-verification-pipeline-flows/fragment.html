Dual-Path Verification & Ephemeral Class Lifecycle

Verification runs through both a deterministic pipeline gate and an agentic assembly review. Diagnostic CSS classes (verify-*, data-insufficient-space) may be baked in during verification and are stripped cleanly in the final --published round.
